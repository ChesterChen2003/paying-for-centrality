Paying for Centrality

Do New York renters pay more for transit access to jobs? Mostly they pay for being central. This atlas follows the analysis from access to rent to who stands to gain from the Interborough Express.

Access: jobs (LEHD LODES 2023) reachable by subway, bus and walking from each tract, departing a weekday 7–8 AM, median of 60 departure minutes; routed with R5 on MTA GTFS (Oct 2026) and OpenStreetMap.

Rent: ACS 2020–24 median gross rent (top-coded at $3,500). Models control for rental-stock age and size, pre-1974 6+ unit share, NYCHA share and borough. Independent academic work using public data; not an MTA product.
